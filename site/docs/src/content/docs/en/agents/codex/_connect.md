## Connecting

1. Install the Codex CLI ([setup guide](https://developers.openai.com/codex/cli/)).
2. In Pleiad, open Settings › "Agents" and press "Recheck status" on the Codex row.
3. Press "Sign in". A sign-in link appears — press "Open the sign-in page" to open your browser and authorize with your ChatGPT account (if the browser does not open, use "Copy link").
4. Wait for it to finish (it times out after about 10 minutes). When the row shows "Signed in", Codex is ready.

- To sign out, press "Sign out" on the Codex row.
- To stop a sign-in in progress, press "Cancel".
