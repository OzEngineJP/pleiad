## Connecting

1. Install Claude Code ([setup guide](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/setup)).
2. In Pleiad, open Settings › "Agents" and press "Recheck status" on the Claude row.
3. Press "Sign in". A sign-in link appears — press "Open the sign-in page" to open your browser and authorize with your Anthropic account (if the browser does not open, use "Copy link").
4. Wait for it to finish (it times out after about 10 minutes). When the row shows "Signed in", Claude is ready.

On first use, Pleiad also needs to fetch the Anthropic Claude Agent SDK. A notice appears on the Claude row — press "Download".

- To sign out, press "Sign out" on the Claude row.
- To stop a sign-in in progress, press "Cancel".
