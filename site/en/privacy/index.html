Privacy policy

Applies to: the Android app “Pleiad” (package name dev.pleiad.android) and the iOS app “Pleiad” (bundle ID dev.pleiad.app)
Effective: October 6, 2026
Updated: November 13, 2026

The Pleiad apps for Android and iOS (“the app”) let you use Pleiad running on your own PC (“the host”) from your phone, through a relay server (“the relay”). You can choose a relay you run yourself or the official relay of Pleiad Plus (“Plus”), run by the developer. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.

1. Where the app sends information

The app sends information off your device to the following places (when you are signed in to Plus, the app also sends the information in section 2 to the developer’s servers).

Your host (your own PC)

The app sends what you do on the host’s screen (conversations, approvals, settings and so on), files you choose to send, your device’s name (on Android, the name in your device settings, or the manufacturer and model; on iOS, the device name) and the app version. These are end-to-end encrypted between your device and your host, and only the host can read them.

The relay you run

A relay you run yourself is operated by you (or the owner of the host), not by the developer. The relay cannot read the content, but to connect the two sides it can see the host and device identifiers, connection tokens, IP addresses, when and how long you connect, and how much data is sent.

The developer’s servers (reading the Pleiad Plus configuration)

The app reads, from the developer’s servers, the configuration that says whether Pleiad Plus is available. It does this even when you are not signed in, sending connection information such as your IP address and the app’s kind and version.

Google’s QR code scanner (Android)

On Android, pairing QR codes are read with the QR code scanner of Google Play services (ML Kit), and images are processed on the device. Google collects device and app information, performance metrics and similar data to diagnose and measure this feature (ML Kit data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy). You can also pair by pasting the code. On iOS, QR codes are read on the device and are not sent anywhere.

Other websites

Links on the host’s screen open in another app, such as your browser. When something shown on the host’s screen loads images or scripts from another website, your device connects to that site directly, and the site sees connection information such as your IP address. Those sites handle data under their own policies.

2. When you sign in to Pleiad Plus

Signing in to Plus is optional. You sign in with a Google or Apple account; sign-in uses Google’s Firebase Authentication, and sign-in information is sent to Google as well. While signed in, the developer’s servers receive:

  • Your email address, user ID and sign-in method
  • Connection information: host and device identifiers, IP addresses, when you connect, and how much data is sent

This information is used to provide the sign-in, relay and sync features. Conversation content between your device and your host is encrypted end to end and cannot be read by the developer.

When you use Plus in Pleiad on your PC, the developer’s servers likewise receive the information above, and each feature sends the following:

  • Host registration: to connect to the official relay, your PC’s identifier, name, OS type and Pleiad version are registered to your account.
  • Sync of settings, skills and instruction files: the content is encrypted on the PC before it is sent, so the developer cannot read it.
  • Automatic delegation routing and voice calls: when you use them through Plus instead of your own key, prompts and audio pass through the developer’s servers to the model provider (they are not covered by end-to-end encryption).

3. How data is protected

Traffic between your device and your host is end-to-end encrypted, and traffic to relays and the Pleiad Plus servers is encrypted as well.

4. Retention and deletion

Once you sign in to Plus, the developer keeps the information in section 2 until you leave Pleiad Plus. You can leave Pleiad Plus from the page opened by “Manage account” in the app. Leaving deletes your connected PCs, synced content and usage records. The account you sign in with (your email address and user ID) remains. Records of leaving and of account operations, and connection records, are kept for a period after you leave. Information stored on your device (paired hosts, keys and settings) is deleted when you remove a host from the list or uninstall the app. The records of a relay you run yourself and the host’s conversation history are kept by the users who run them, and they can delete them.

5. AI services used by the host

The host uses AI services that you signed in to with your own accounts (such as Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity) from your PC. Instructions and conversations you send from the app may be passed to those services through the host. Those services handle data under their own policies.

6. Contact

Contact us through GitHub Issues or the developer contact on the app’s Google Play or App Store listing.
